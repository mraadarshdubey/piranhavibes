How it's made

Three things we
refuse to compromise on

01

The fabric

Bio-washed, pre-shrunk combed cotton. Soft on day one, still soft after fifty washes — never stiff, never scratchy.

02

The print

Every Marathi line is hand-set as original typography, then cured so the colour locks into the weave instead of sitting on top of it.

03

The meaning

Nothing is printed for the sake of a trend. Every slogan is a phrase a Marathi household actually says — that's the whole point.
